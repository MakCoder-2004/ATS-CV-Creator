# Personal-local posting source policy (#103)

**Decision:** For the maintainer's own local CV Tailor installation, allow **Lever and Greenhouse** to become eligible for future automatic intake through their documented, public, exact-posting API routes. This is an explicit product-risk decision for personal use, **not a claim of a publisher license or legal advice**. The maintainer has no written platform or employer grant and does not want a permission-outreach prerequisite for this local workflow. The earlier [source-rights research](../research/source-access-retention-gates-2026-10-26.md) remains the evidence for what each platform's published materials do and do not say.

| Source | Local decision |
| --- | --- |
| Lever | May become eligible for a named published posting through the documented Postings API. Public readability and the API's third-party-scraping language do not themselves grant general redistribution rights. |
| Greenhouse | May become eligible for a named posting on a public employer board through the documented Job Board API. Its careers-page documentation does not establish broader reuse rights. |
| LinkedIn and Indeed | **Remain paused** for automatic reads, indexed fallback and fetched-text saving. Their reviewed access restrictions or lack of an approved selected-job route are not overridden by personal-use intent. Manual paste and opening the original remain available. |

**Local data boundary.** A fetched result stays temporary until the person checks source, title, employer, exact role, completeness and freshness, then explicitly chooses Save. Only the accepted posting and its separate source/provenance snapshot may enter Saved jobs and the local workspace. It may be used for that person's CV tailoring only after the usual separate, run-scoped outgoing-data review and model authorization. Never send profile/CV data in job searches; never use login cookies, bypass challenges, scrape bulk listings, reroute a blocked host through another provider, or silently refresh a posting. Keep retrieval time and source-reported update time visible. Refresh is an explicit action through the same eligible route; an old snapshot is not automatically current.

**Retention and deletion.** Accepted postings and source history remain local until the person explicitly deletes the saved job. Current-workspace deletion must remove its attributable posting, source snapshot and derived draft data according to the existing deletion contract. User-created backup files are separate artifacts: older backups may retain and later restore deleted text, and the app does not silently rewrite or destroy those files. Disclose this at review/deletion. Historical saved jobs, drafts, approvals and backups are preserved; this decision is not a migration or erasure instruction. No fetched preview rejected or left unaccepted may enter jobs, backups, CVs or a durable provider cache.

**Still blocked in the shipped app.** The [current workflow](../workflows.md) keeps Lever, LinkedIn and Indeed automatic reads paused under #104; no Greenhouse automatic-save route is approved by this document alone. Do not re-enable any automatic read/save/refresh until #102 establishes the strict OS boundary, constrained provider/network broker, cache cleanup and lifecycle; #97–#99 add exact-role contracts, safe adapters, visible review/save and focused regression tests (including formerly paused adapter safety checks); and #101 covers separately approved installed Windows validation. The observed fixed AppContainer+Job child is only one compatibility step, not the network/file isolation gate. For public-repo builds, leave this future route off by default on other installations unless a person explicitly opts into the same personal-local scope with a source and backup-retention notice; broader distribution requires a fresh product-policy decision. This maintainer choice is not a default license or promise for other users. Reassess if terms, scope, distribution, storage, or source access changes.
