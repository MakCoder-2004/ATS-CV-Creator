# Direct public posting intake: temporary research policy (source branch)

This source-branch validation note is not release or installed-app evidence. The renderer's Save job control and research-handoff callback still need the separately owned UI change; main-process refusal is already enforced. No external posting text or live network requests were used in the synthetic checks.

For a non-Lever public job URL, the bounded direct adapter selects a canonical role identity. A full exact-role capture with a named employer displays title, company, and complete text in the temporary preview; partial, blocked, ambiguous, or employerless captures display a research notice instead. Both full and incomplete completed reads retain their original `JobResearchRun` in the main process for at most 15 minutes before handoff. A per-token bounded timer aborts and deletes the temporary capture at expiry even if no later API call occurs; cancel, replacement and app close clear temporary data. `takePostingResearchRun(token)` clones and validates the run once, checks selected URL/provider identity, expiry, cancellation and replacement, and cannot take a Lever result. At handoff the entire posting capture is discarded, including its preview, edits, review and timer; the posting token is no longer available. The existing named `job-research:from-posting` IPC creates a separate volatile research session that owns the run for up to 15 minutes without a second GET. Full direct evidence can be promoted to reviewed full scope without Tavily; indexed search for incomplete evidence remains a separate explicit action. Neither initial read nor transfer dispatches search or Pi.

**Storage rule:** new non-Lever fetched results (`linkedin-public-html-v1`, `public-jsonld-v1`) cannot be accepted as saved jobs or refreshed into an existing saved job, even after preview edits and review. Trusted main returns “Review temporarily or paste text manually.” No new direct result enters `jobs`, backup, posting analysis cache/state, draft, consent, or recovery through the legacy acceptance API. User-pasted manual jobs and pre-existing Lever acceptance/refresh remain available. Historical saved non-Lever snapshots restored from backups remain readable and backed up, but their fetched source cannot be refreshed into a new revision. An intentional manual paste is independent of fetching and does not authorize retention of a fetched source. Direct findings and URLs expire on cancel, replacement, app close or TTL; no generated CV or export is authorized from the rights-pending temporary session.

Focused synthetic evidence: `tests/universal-direct-workbench.test.ts` asserts the temporary-only full preview notice, server-side refusal before and after review or handoff, no saved job/backup content, one direct GET through single-use transfer and posting-token deletion, timer-driven expiry without another API call, and full reviewed scope without search/model dispatch; it also exercises an inert JSON-LD extraction worker. `tests/job-research-transfer.test.ts` checks incomplete findings, one-GET handoff, explicit exact-ID indexed verification reread, invalid/cancelled/replaced tokens and transport failure. Existing `tests/jobs.test.ts` covers Lever and manual save/refresh. No full suite, GUI, packaging, provider request or installed verification was run on this branch.

Open gates: renderer hides Save job and uses the named token transfer, outgoing-data disclosure and explicit run consent for volatile role-only analysis, temporary analysis not written to SQLite or backups, no generation/export until retention rights are verified, integration review, worker-copy/build/desktop IPC checks and installed proof. Future verified licensing or retention rights require a new explicit product decision; technical public access alone is not permission.
