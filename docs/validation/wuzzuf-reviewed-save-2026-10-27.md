In-place upgrades retain authorization; moving/copying the app to another root does not. Restart CV Tailor after creating or removing the file. The app never creates it. Conflicts, missing evidence, login/restricted pages, and incomplete/teaser text fail closed. Static parsing cannot evaluate arbitrary external CSS/media queries; this residual limitation is documented, and the UI explicitly requires the human to inspect the original page and compare its visible title, employer and complete text before Save.
