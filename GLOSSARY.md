# CV Tailor

CV Tailor is a personal workspace for organizing career evidence and preparing a role-specific CV from a saved job posting. Its language distinguishes a person's source facts, the posting being targeted and the document prepared for review.

## Language

**Career fact**:
A factual record of a person's experience, education, skill or accomplishment that can support CV content.
_Avoid_: Claim (unless referring to a statement in a draft)

**Evidence**:
A career fact or source record that supports a statement in a tailored CV.
_Avoid_: Proof (evidence can support a statement without guaranteeing its truth)

**Job posting**:
The original description of a role the person is considering.
_Avoid_: Job (when referring specifically to the saved source text), Vacancy

**Tailored CV**:
A CV draft arranged and worded for a specific job posting, using the person's career facts as its source.
_Avoid_: Resume (unless quoting the user's term), Generated CV (implies every part was generated)

**Draft**:
A not-yet-approved version of a tailored CV.
_Avoid_: Final CV

**Review**:
The person's assessment of draft wording, supporting evidence and readiness to use the tailored CV.
_Avoid_: Verification (unless a specific verification operation is meant)

**Approval**:
The person's explicit acceptance of a reviewed tailored CV for export or use.
_Avoid_: Validation (means checking conditions, not the person's decision)

**Saved job**:
A job posting retained in the workspace for later editing or tailoring.
_Avoid_: Saved posting (use “job posting” in explanatory prose)

**Profile**:
The organized collection of a person's career facts and supporting evidence.
_Avoid_: CV (the profile is source material, not the tailored document)

**Source**:
The original record or passage from which a career fact or job requirement is drawn.
_Avoid_: Citation (unless a formal citation is actually provided)
