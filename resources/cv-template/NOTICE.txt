The supplied Overleaf template identifies its author as Audric Serador,
credits inspiration to https://github.com/sb2nov/resume, and declares MIT.
The supplied resume.tex, original custom commands and section fixtures retain
their layout. An additional linked-project heading command supports the user's
requested right-aligned project hyperlinks, wrapping long names/tools safely.
Application-generated content replaces the section files; education CGPA uses
the original subheading's second-row right column. For extended Latin content, compilation selects T1 font encoding
to preserve accented characters in PDF text extraction; layout macros, margins,
font family and document size remain those of the supplied template.
